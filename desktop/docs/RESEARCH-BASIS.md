# Research basis

The War Room's KPI model follows the Aftergraph Intelligence Systems research program rather than generic vanity metrics.

Core outcome metrics represented in the UI:
- Verified Success Rate (VSR)
- False Completion Rate (FCR)
- Constraint Retention Rate (CRR)
- Unauthorized Action Rate (UAR)
- Evidence Completeness
- Time to Verified Outcome (TVO)
- Cost Per Verified Outcome (CPVO)
- Control Plane Tax (CPT)

The operator model follows the program principle that declared completion is not equivalent to verified outcome, that evidence must remain source-labelled, and that human-facing state should prioritize goal, progress, needs-you, cost, risk and outcome.

AIE influence is expressed through the five institution strip surfaces: Identity, Authority, Policy, Budgets and Evidence. The app does not claim to implement the whole AIE standard; it exposes those governance dimensions as observable operator surfaces.
